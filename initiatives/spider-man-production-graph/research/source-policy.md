# Source and extraction policy

Record the exact URL, publisher, source tier and type, access date, and the
smallest useful supporting excerpt or locator. Do not archive copyrighted pages
wholesale. An inaccessible source remains in the registry with its access
status and can support no new extraction until reviewed.

Automated extraction must identify its tool and version in an extraction
manifest. Manual transcription must identify the reviewer. Transformations
from source text to normalized roles must remain reversible through the claim
and evidence records.

Search results are discovery aids, not independent sources. Prefer the linked
publisher page. Public databases can seed the credit inventory but do not prove
that the inventory matches the final on-screen roll.

Rejected claims belong in `research/ledger.md` with a reason; they should not be
quietly lost and must not be emitted into graph exports.
