# Expansion playbook

1. Register candidate sources before extracting claims.
2. Inventory source-native credit strings without normalizing away detail.
3. Create or reuse stable entities; record aliases and unresolved identities.
4. Add credited-role edges and claim-level evidence.
5. Map roles to teams and departments without assuming employer relationships.
6. Research production-specific assets, processes, tools, locations, vendors,
   and handoffs; distinguish evidence from industry patterns.
7. Run validation, duplicates, coverage, SQLite build, and exports.
8. Review all disputed and low-confidence claims before publication.

For a new department, first populate its leadership and a representative
subteam, then test one complete deliverable path. For a new production, mint a
new production ID and reuse shared role, process, company, and tool entities
only when they are genuinely the same concept.
