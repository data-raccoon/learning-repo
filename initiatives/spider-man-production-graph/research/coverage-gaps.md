# Coverage gaps

The seed is intentionally a schema-and-pilot slice, not a complete credit roll.

- Public databases may omit, reorder, or change credits and may not match the
  released on-screen roll.
- The pilot contains only a representative sample of production-design and art
  department personnel.
- Named vendors, locations, software, and production-specific deliverables need
  corroborating public evidence before population.
- Most process nodes are industry-pattern hypotheses. Production-specific
  interviews, art books, guild features, and vendor breakdowns are needed to
  promote them to inferred or verified claims.
- Reporting completeness is measured against the discovered public inventory,
  not against an unknown true total.
